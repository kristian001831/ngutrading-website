What Is NGU Edge?

NGU Edge is a structured performance operating system for traders.

Not just a journal. Not just a P&L tracker. Not just a prop dashboard. It is a discipline and profitability optimization framework built around execution.

Most traders measure profit. Professionals measure execution. Profit becomes a byproduct of structure.
