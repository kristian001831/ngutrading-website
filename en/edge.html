What is NGU Edge?

NGU Edge is a structured performance operating system for traders. It tracks how you trade, not just what you make.

Core philosophy: most traders measure profit; professionals measure execution.

Everything below is live

Structured Journal

Per-trade and per-day logging, emotional tags (max 2/day), execution quality, notes, daily scoring.

P&L Calendar + Accounts

Track evaluation, funded, and personal accounts with score context and monthly net view.

Prop + Capital System

Attempts, payouts, challenge spend, net after fees, pass rate, first payout rate, capital efficiency.

90-day Seasons

XP progression, clean-day count, streaks, trend indicator, structured leaderboard.

League Progression

Rookie → Apprentice → Competent → Skilled → Advanced → Master → Legend (+ I/II/III).

Execution Intelligence

Execution score, stability score, clean days, trend state, behavior leaks.

Drawdown Risk Insight

Depth, recovery behavior, risk exposure trend tracking.

Goal System

1 financial + 1 behavioral goal per season with progress bars and auto completion.

90-day Forecast

Data-aware modeling based on account size and structure (copy vs independent).

Shareable Performance Card

League, XP, streak, execution score, clean days, badges.
