NGU Trader Coach App

NGU Trader Coach is a web-based analytics and journaling app for prop traders who want clarity instead of hype.

This app is separate from the NGU Coach Bot and the NGU Trading Coach program — they are not the same.

What it does
  • Tracks evaluations, funded accounts, payouts, and all prop-related costs.
  • Shows real pass rates, failure rates, and true net profitability.
  • Journals execution: rules, consistency, and emotional patterns.
  • Turns data into personalized insights so you know what to fix next.
What it is not
  • No signals.
  • No broker connections.
  • No strategy selling.

Just honest numbers, discipline, and decision-making support for serious traders.

Access & roadmap

The app is free for now, but servers are still small, so it may slow down when too many people visit.

Next up: a public beta on stronger servers, followed later by a free plan and a premium plan with premium features.
