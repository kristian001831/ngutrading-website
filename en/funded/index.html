Read carefully

What usually matters more than the front-page promise.

Drawdown modeStatic, trailing, intraday, and end-of-day are not interchangeable. They change trader behavior.
Payout cadenceThe public split number matters less than how fast and how often you can realistically access payouts.
Format complexityMore plan choice is useful only if you can still understand which route actually fits your style.
Choose Alpha if

You are futures-first, want cleaner structure, and prefer a rule set that feels more operational than promotional.

Choose FundedHero if

You want the easiest first prop test, lower friction, and a more flexible retail-style funded route.

Choose FH Futures if

You trade futures, want plan variety, and need to compare multiple route shapes before choosing one.
