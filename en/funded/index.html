Read carefully

What usually matters more than the front-page promise.

01

Drawdown mode

Static, trailing, intraday and end-of-day are not interchangeable — they change trader behaviour.

02

Payout cadence

The split number matters less than how fast and how often you can realistically access payouts.

03

Format complexity

More plan choice only helps if you can still understand which route fits your style.
