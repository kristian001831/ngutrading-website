Complete NGU Edge system breakdown

1) Structured trading journal

Per-trade logging, daily performance logging, emotional tagging (max 2 per day), execution quality tracking, and notes/observations. Each day receives an execution score. A green PnL day can score low if rules were broken, and a red PnL day can score high if rules were respected. The system separates outcome from behavior.

2) P&L calendar & account tracking

Track multiple evaluations, funded accounts, and personal accounts with monthly net result visibility, profit-vs-loss day patterns, and average execution score overlays.

3) Prop firm & capital system

Tracks evaluation attempts, funded accounts, payouts, total challenge spend, net profitability after fees, pass rate, first payout rate, and Capital Efficiency score.

Capital Efficiency = how effectively prop capital is converted into real retained profit.

4) 90-day season system

Performance is structured in 90-day cycles with XP progression, stability tracking, clean-day count, streak tracking, execution trend indicators, and season leaderboard logic. Seasons reset. Discipline compounds.

5) League progression

Rookie → Apprentice → Competent → Skilled → Advanced → Master → Legend, each with I, II, III sub-levels. Progression is based on execution quality, stability, clean-day consistency, capital behavior, and structured activity. Transparent, but not exploitable.

6) Execution intelligence

Tracks execution score, stability score, clean days, drawdown risk insight, and execution trend (Improving / Stable / Declining). Identifies green-to-red days, overtrading clusters, rule-violation patterns, and emotional breakdown sequences.

7) Drawdown risk insight

Analyzes drawdown depth, recovery behavior, and risk exposure trends to build awareness before accounts are blown.

8) Goal system

Each season includes one financial goal and one behavioral goal, with progress bars, auto-completion upon achievement, and proper reset between seasons.

9) 90-day forecast

Forecast logic considers account size, funded accounts, evaluations, capital structure, and copy-vs-independent mode to simulate structured growth potential without fantasy projections.

10) Shareable performance card

Shareable cards include league level, XP, streak, execution score, clean days, and badge count. It promotes discipline, not PnL flexing.

FAQ
Is NGU Edge a signal platform?

No. NGU Edge is a structured execution and performance operating system focused on behavior, discipline, and measurable process quality.

Does NGU Edge separate behavior from outcome?

Yes. A profitable day can still receive a low execution score if rules were broken, while a losing day can score high when structure was respected.

Where does the NGU Strategy Coach fit?

It is an included tool under NGU Edge, used for structured audits and rule enforcement assistance. It is not positioned as a standalone product page.

What is the role of NGU Community and NGU Advanced?

Community is the free access layer. NGU Advanced — Operator Level is a paid layer that builds on NGU Edge with deeper implementation support and resources.
