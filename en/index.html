Main Product
NGU Edge

NGU Edge is a structured performance operating system for traders. It is not just a journal, not just a P&L tracker, and not just a prop dashboard. It is built to measure execution quality, discipline, and long-term profitability behavior.

Most traders measure profit. Professionals measure execution. NGU Edge tracks how you trade — not only what you make.

Core Philosophy
Profit is the output. Structure is the driver.

Measurable Discipline

Stability, clean days, rule adherence, and emotional consistency are measured directly.

Behavior Over Outcome

A green day can still score poorly if execution fails. A red day can still score high when rules are respected.

Compounding Structure

NGU Edge is designed for sustainable profitability, not short-term P&L dopamine.

What NGU Edge already does
Live capabilities

1) Structured Trading Journal

Log per-trade and per-day activity, emotional tags (2/day), notes, and execution quality with daily scoring.

2) P&L Calendar & Account Tracking

Track multiple accounts (evaluation, funded, personal) with profit/loss days, average score, and monthly net result.

3) Prop Firm & Capital System

Track attempts, funded accounts, payouts, challenge costs, pass rate, first payout rate, and net profitability after fees.

4) 90-Day Season System

Structured cycles include XP progression, clean-day count, streaks, trend indicator, and leaderboard resets.

5) League Progression

Rookie to Legend with I/II/III sub-levels, based on execution quality, stability, and disciplined activity.

6) Execution Intelligence

Tracks execution/stability scores, clean days, trend state, and structure leaks like overtrading or violation clusters.

7) Drawdown Risk Insight

Analyzes drawdown depth, recovery behavior, and risk exposure trends before account failure.

8) Goal System

One financial + one behavioral goal per season with auto-complete progress and clean reset logic.

9) 90-Day Forecast

Account-size aware modeling using funded/evaluation structure with copy-trading vs independent simulation.

10) Shareable Performance Card

Share league level, XP, streak, execution score, clean days, and badge count — discipline over flexing.

NGU Edge Product Roadmap
From discipline to full profitability optimization
No hype. Clear evolution. Built layer by layer.

Current State — Live Foundation

Execution journal, season cycles, league progression, goal system, capital efficiency tracking, 90-day forecast, and shareable performance card are operational.

Phase 2 — Optimization Refinement (3–6 months)

Sharper behavior detection, emotional correlation insight upgrades, better forecast modeling, weekly summaries, and drawdown recovery analytics.

Multi-account scaling planner, risk/failure probability modeling, capital allocation optimization, and deep multi-season archives.

12–24 Month Vision

NGU Edge evolves into a performance hub, discipline benchmark, social execution network, and capital optimization engine.

FAQ
Is NGU Edge just a trading journal?

No. It includes journal structure, but the core is execution measurement, behavioral intelligence, and capital efficiency tracking.

No. The system is discipline-first and data-grounded. It does not market fantasy outcomes.
