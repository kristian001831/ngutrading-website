# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Independent retail traders and aspiring funded traders who are serious enough to want structure, but skeptical of loud trading marketing. They arrive looking for a calmer environment, practical next steps, community access, and a sense that someone here actually understands execution, discipline, and capital behavior.

## Product Purpose
NGU Trading is a dark bilingual marketing surface for a trading ecosystem. Its job is to introduce the NGU world, invite the right people into the community, explain the deeper operator layer, and help traders find practical routes toward getting funded. Success looks like trust, curiosity, and voluntary interaction, not hard-sell conversion pressure.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, exacting, magnetic. The tone should feel composed, observant, and slightly obsessive in a good way. It should feel more like stepping into a disciplined night desk than entering a hype funnel.

## Anti-references
Generic fintech navy-and-gold landing pages, fake scarcity countdowns, glassy SaaS card grids, crypto-neon dashboards, motivational-trader macho branding, and any surface that looks like an AI-generated conversion template.

## Design Principles
The site should invite depth, not pressure.
Discipline should feel tactile and real, not abstract and corporate.
Interaction should reward curiosity with clarity.
Every section should reduce noise and increase trust.
Distinctiveness matters more than feature-volume.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Meet a solid WCAG AA baseline. Preserve keyboard clarity, visible focus states, readable contrast, and reduced-motion respect. Mobile behavior matters because many visitors will first see the site on a phone. Motion should feel ambient, not necessary for comprehension.
