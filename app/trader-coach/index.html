NGU Trader Coach
APP DETAILS
Clarity over hype for prop traders.

NGU Trader Coach is a web-based analytics and journaling app for prop traders who want clarity instead of hype. It tracks evaluations, funded accounts, payouts, and all prop-related costs to show real pass rates, failure rates, and true net profitability.

Launch NGU Trader Coach → Run Behavior Diagnostic Quiz → Back to app home

No signals, no broker connections, no strategy selling — just execution and behavior data.

What it actually does

Lifecycle tracking

Evaluations, funded accounts, payouts, and prop costs in one clean timeline.

True profitability

Net profit after fees, resets, and failed attempts — no vanity stats.

Pass/fail clarity

See real pass rates and failure reasons across accounts, firms, and time.

Execution + behavior insights

The app focuses on execution and behavior — journaling trades, rule adherence, consistency, and emotional patterns — and turns that data into personalized insights that explain why accounts pass or fail and what to improve next.

Trade journaling

Capture rationale, rule compliance, and emotions with each entry.

Personalized insights

Identify the behaviors blocking payouts and get precise next-step fixes.

Access status

Upcoming rollout

Public beta with stronger servers is next, followed by free + premium plans.

Not the same thing as the Coach Bot

NGU Trader Coach (the app) is separate from the NGU Coach Bot. The app focuses on analytics + journaling, while the Coach Bot is a chat-based assistant for execution feedback.
